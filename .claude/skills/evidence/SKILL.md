# Skill: Evidence

## Purpose
A confident wrong fact costs more than an honest "I don't know". This skill
makes the status of each claim visible.

## Labels
- **Verified**: you or the owner read it at the source. Say where and when
  (file and line, URL and date, command and output).
- **Not confirmed**: a claim from research, a summary or memory that nobody read
  at the source. Keep the label until it is checked.
- **Contradicted**: the source says something different. Say what it says.
- **New**: something found that nobody expected.

## Rules
- Research tells you what to check. Verification checks it. They are not the
  same step.
- Never invent a column name, URL, number, license or date. If it does not
  exist or is dirty, document that.
- Report test results as they are: if something failed or was skipped, say so.
- Do not describe a check as passed unless you saw it pass.
- Mark simulated or synthetic data as such, everywhere it appears.
- Evidence is dated: facts about external systems can change.
